# Mayor and Vice Mayor

The **Mayor** is the chief executive of **the Municipality of Minalabac**. The Mayor is elected by residents in the **Philippine National and Local Elections**, held every three years, and is responsible for carrying out the programs, policies and ordinances approved by the **Sangguniang Bayan (Municipal Council)**.

# Invite the Mayor to Your Event

Residents, schools and organizations may invite the Mayor to community activities by sending a formal letter to the **Office of the Mayor** with:

- Name of organization
- Event title
- Date and time
- Event location
- Contact person and details
